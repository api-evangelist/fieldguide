Always know where your engagements stand For 30 years, engagement work has run on two surfaces: the binder and the sheet. Both are precise, both are auditable, and neither was built to answer the question a manager asks more than any other, which is where the engagement stands right now. Answering that from a binder or a sheet means filtering, sorting, and reconciling against memory or a side tracker.
