Fieldguide is an AI-native platform for audit and advisory firms, providing professional-grade "Field Agents" that plan, execute, and document engagement work end-to-end across financial audit, SOC audits, IT audit, risk advisory, tax, cybersecurity, and regulatory compliance engagements. The platform pairs engagement management, document management, insights and analytics, and a client hub with an open REST API (api.fieldguide.io) that exposes companies, engagements, requests, sheets, files, comments, milestones, users, insights, and webhook subscriptions. Fieldguide is used by half of the top 100 firms, is SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO/IEC 42001 certified, and is backed by 8VC and Bessemer Venture Partners.

**Areas:** Company · Audit · Advisory · Accounting · Compliance · Risk Management · Engagement Management · Artificial Intelligence · Agents · Webhook

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Fieldguide: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
